Why this is defensive AI

  • Threat: a buyer submits a convincing but inaccurate payment claim to obtain goods.
  • AI today: Tesseract performs OCR on image uploads when the Python backend is available.
  • Trusted control: deterministic rules cross-check extracted fields with a synthetic ledger.
  • Human decision: the merchant is warned, not automatically charged or accused.

What Exhibit P does not claim

A matched demo record is not proof that money arrived. Only an authorized bank or payment-provider record can support real settlement confirmation. Image forgery detection, real payment APIs and production false-positive rates remain future work.

Offline browser mode uses known synthetic receipt fields. Flask mode performs genuine OCR if Tesseract is installed.

EXHIBIT P · Week 3 hackathon PoC · All names, receipts and payments are synthetic. No customer funds or real payment-provider data are accessed.
